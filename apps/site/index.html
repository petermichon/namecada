Namecada

Check a name across the open web.

Domains, package registries and handles, checked in one pass. Around 140 sources, each verdict landing as it resolves.

Download for Android All releases

Signed APK. Desktop builds for Linux, Windows and macOS are published on the same releases page.

  • On your device. Every check runs locally, so there is no server to log your searches.
  • No account, no tracking. Recent names and verdicts stay in local storage on your machine.
  • Open source. MIT OR Apache-2.0. Every source, its request and the rule behind its verdict are documented in the repository.

A verdict is a snapshot, not a promise: availability does not mean you can register the name, and a domain row reports registry state. Re-check at the source before committing to anything.
